# Hebei legacy vocabulary audit

Five public community-mirror Hebei Education Press books (English, 衔接三年级起点) are pinned to TapXWorld/ChinaTextbook commit 5a80345f2043ba6f8db8d7be9cf3db82725ff1f7, upstream R-ED-B::002–006 at bf15137b2f36f45e08e7ac4a77956fc2ccd1c215, and complete parent SHA-256 values. All 5 observed cover ISBNs pass ISBN13 checksums. Cover approval stamps are 2012 for grade 7 and 2013 for grades 8–9. These legacy books establish no 2024 current-edition or city/cohort adoption fact. The cached full parents omit copyright/imprint pages (title directly followed by preface); first-edition, copyright and printing dates remain unknown. Grade 9 explicitly has one whole-year book. The audited frontmatter names the curriculum standard generically; this audit does not infer its precise version year.

There are 2,552 retained occurrences across 42 literal units: 1,850 Vocabulary(I) primary lexical entries plus 702 separately labelled Structures and Expressions entries. Per book totals are 7 upper 529 (375 + 154), 7 lower 499 (354 + 145), 8 upper 408 (304 + 104), 8 lower 484 (357 + 127), 9 whole 632 (460 + 172). Both appendix types retain their own list_kind. Repeated printed occurrences are preserved; this is not a unique-lemma count. Alphabetical Vocabulary(II), grade 7 upper primary-school word table, and irregular-verb tables are retained but not double-counted.

All 107 retained pages were rendered and viewed, including cover/preface figures, title pages and complete lexical appendices. Five combined identity/vocabulary PDFs were derived using original doc.select. Every retained page passed RGB equality at scale 0.45 and complete native-text equality against its parent. The fragments total 7,094,011 bytes; no full-parent body or grammar-page visual audit is claimed. Exact parent-to-retained PDF page mapping is in each integrity-and-review.json and aggregate derivative-fidelity.json.

Curated fields are primary headword or complete printed expression, literal unit, appendix kind, source PDF and printed appendix page. IPA, Chinese gloss, word class, bold status, alternatives and parenthetical expansions are uncurated/null. The source legend calls bold words required and other words contact vocabulary in these books; it is not a 2022/2025 national-list or local-exam classification. Native control/private-use glyphs must not be treated as verified pronunciation text. Grade 7 lower was fully transcribed by visual reading because its appendix Latin text decodes as control codes. A manual source-page/unit TSV is retained. OCR outputs were temporary aids only and are excluded.

The other 4 books were crosschecked against the independently extracted alphabetical primary labels. After excluding one indented fridge-gloss word-class continuation, the primary-label sets match apart from a genuine grade 7 upper source conflict: main unit-order Niagara Falls (PDF 140), alphabetical Niagra Falls (PDF 144). Main spelling is retained; the source index is not silently corrected. Present has two separate unit-order occurrences and one combined alphabetical entry. Additional visible punctuation, interleaved pronunciation and POS suffix corrections are recorded.
