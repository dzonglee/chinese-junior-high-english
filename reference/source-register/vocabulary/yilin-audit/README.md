# Yilin legacy wordlist audit

Six cached public community-mirror books have 2,293 literal unit-list entries across 44 unit groups. Counts include repeated headwords and separately printed phrases; they are not unique lemmas. All 32 unit-list pages were rendered and visually checked against the native extraction. All 54 additional alphabetical, Level II and proper-name pages were rendered and inspected for section identity and completeness, retained as evidence, and are not included in the unit-list count. Thirty-six identity pages were visually inspected, including source covers, title, copyright, preface, contents and back covers.

The source wordlist legend excludes instructions, grammar explanations, study skills and Project words; unit lists therefore are not an exhaustive list of every book token. Its star means recognition and listening comprehension are required but spelling is not required. Individual star flags, IPA, Chinese glosses and variant annotations are null because they were not separately transcribed. Do not confuse these stars with the national standard level-2 marks. Literal source headwords, including American variants in this generally British family, are preserved.

`unit-wordlist.json` in each book directory contains the curated literal entry/unit/headword/page data. `raw-native-lines.json` is uncurated supporting extraction, including unreliable phonetic and Chinese font encodings. Do not treat the raw native lines as audited IPA/gloss data. `visual-corrections.json` records excluded inline annotations and corrected interword spacing. The USA expansion continuation America is not counted as a separate unit entry; inline comparative base form far is not counted. Further/farther and furthest/farthest are combined exactly as the single printed list rows, with their semicolon retained.

`parents.json` pins each full cached parent by bytes and SHA-256, retaining the existing R-ED-B source/manifest and immutable GitHub acquisition URL. These mirrors have not been independently matched to publisher originals. Source bibliographic observations are separately recorded in `bibliography.json`, with actual page evidence and valid ISBN-13 checksums. The observed edition statements are second editions, not claims about first editions; all first-edition dates remain unknown. A 2021 printing is not a current 2024 curriculum revision. Approval covers are 2012 for grade 7 and 2013 for grades 8–9; grade 9 copyright/edition statements are 2014.

Eighteen complete-page evidence PDFs cover unit lists, additional indexes/supplements and identity pages. They were derived from original parents using doc.select. The catalog StructTreeRoot and Outlines entries were removed because stale accessibility and navigation references kept all omitted full-book pages/resources alive. All other preserved catalog display/color/OCG behavior and page content remain unchanged. `fidelity-check.json` records exact RGB equality at scale 0.6 and exact extracted-native-text equality for every one of the 122 retained pages. Source visual review used scale 1.8. This does not claim a full-book accessibility repair or a full-book content audit.

All material is research reference only; commercial_reuse_approved is false. City adoption, current-cohort applicability and local annual exam vocabulary remain unknown. No city-year coverage upgrade follows from this edition audit. No audio was acquired.
