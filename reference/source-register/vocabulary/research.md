Every retained source artifact has manifest bytes, SHA-256 and an opened-check scope. Purpose-limited
query projections explicitly pin original response bytes/hashes and identify their transformation;
they are not described as original articles or downloaded lexical files. Unrelated public-case
branches, advertisements, duplicate raw/decoded bodies and challenge HTML are excluded where noted.

were made after its automation restrictions were inspected. Guangzhou's practice RAR was statically
opened, never executed; zero media sets were independently played or decoded.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
node scripts/check-register.mjs reference/source-register/vocabulary
node reference/source-register/vocabulary/check-audit.mjs
```

The checks validate schema, hydrated bytes/hashes, original-to-registered lexical/group/page
sequences, counts, visible ISBN checksums and explicit applicability/reuse flags. They do not repeat
visual review, establish publisher authenticity or licensing, or make the unit complete.
