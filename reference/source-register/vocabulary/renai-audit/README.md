# Renai legacy vocabulary audit

Research only; no commercial permission or release approval. Six community-mirror Project English books are pinned to TapXWorld/ChinaTextbook commit 5a80345f2043ba6f8db8d7be9cf3db82725ff1f7 and complete parent SHA-256 values. Their preface/postscript basis is the 2011 English standard. These books are not 2024 new editions and establish no city/cohort adoption.

There are 2,634 printed unit-list occurrences across 86 literal unit/topic/review groups: grade 7 upper 410, grade 7 lower 484, grade 8 upper 499, grade 8 lower 424, grade 9 upper 521, grade 9 lower 296. Counts preserve repeated entries and phrases and are not unique-word or lemma counts. The alphabetical indexes duplicate the same book vocabulary and are retained separately, not double-counted. Supplementary attached wordlists, names, place names, spelling variants, irregular verbs and final cumulative phrase tables are retained as PDFs, not merged into primary unit occurrences. The attached-list legend explicitly says reference only, not required mastery.

The curated fields are primary printed headword or phrase, unit/topic/review group, source PDF page and printed appendix page. Unicode NFKC converts full-width native Latin; visible word gaps and interrupted-IPA phrases were visually corrected. Per-entry bold status, IPA, Chinese gloss, part of speech and parenthetical alternatives/equal-sign expansions are deliberately unknown/unreviewed rather than guessed. Complete source PDFs retain them. The legacy legend identifies bold as curriculum-required and roman as text-understanding; it does not establish a 2022/2025 national mapping or current local exam priority. Native IPA has private-use glyphs and must not be treated as a pronunciation lexicon.

All 154 retained source PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected, including cover figures, copyright/title pages and complete appendices. Four derivatives per book retain identity, unit list, alphabetical index and supplementary appendices. All 24 derivatives were opened and all 154 pages compared to the original parent: RGB raster equality at scale 0.45 and complete native text equality passed. Original doc.select preserves the parent catalog and color handling. PDF processing emits source-object/xref warnings; the warnings ledger records them, and selected-page equality does not certify repair of the complete parent.

All six ISBNs were visually read on wraparound cover/copyright and pass ISBN13 checksums. These copies have 2021 printings. Grade 9 lower contains a genuine source conflict: copyright first-edition 2014-11 while the same page CIP record says 2014.10. Both are retained. Catalogue/adoption/year applicability remain unknown. All commercial_reuse_approved flags are false; the book copyright notice explicitly prohibits making publications without authorization.
