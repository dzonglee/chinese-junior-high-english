## What the evidence establishes

All 20 items carry 1.5 marks, for 30 marks. Section subtotals of 7.5/15/7.5 are arithmetic, not separately printed claims. The 2023 third-section instruction calls its stimulus **对话**; the other four years call it **短文**. The record preserves that wording rather than inferring what an unavailable recording contains.

`listening-task-specs.json` cites the exact printed pages. No recording, transcript, pause schedule, total listening duration or speaking rubric was obtained. These are provincial-paper models, with `cities: []`: hosting on Fuzhou or Quanzhou education websites does not establish any priority city's use of the paper.

Each retained Fujian PDF contains the official cover and three whole listening-question pages. The manifest preserves original URL, parent hash, parent page numbers and R-SOUTH source ID. No duplicate original download or independent corroboration is claimed. Liaoning's 2021 original scheme, FAQ and cohort reply (R-LS::001–003) establish the provincial framework of 90 written plus 30 listening/oral from autumn-2021 grade-7 entrants, with municipal implementation. For province-set papers it specifies 120 marks including 30 listening marks, a 90-minute whole-English sitting and approximately 20 minutes listening. Its explicit five-year validity from August 2018 prevents treating it as a current 2024–2026 rule without an extension. Annual 2023–2025 schedules (R-LS::009–011) separately verify 08:30–10:00 whole-English sittings for cities using province-set papers; they do not establish a 90-minute listening/oral test or name all participating cities.
