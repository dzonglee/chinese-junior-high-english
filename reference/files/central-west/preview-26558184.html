Do you know China's Three-Year Weight Management（体重管理） Plan？It asks us to have a healthy eating habit.Here is some advice about healthy eating habits.
Eat breakfast. Breakfast is the most important meal of a day.Breakfast gives you energy for the morning.And if you have a balanced breakfast，you will eat less during the day.Eggs and milk are good choices for breakfast.
Listen to your body. You may feel hungry in the afternoon and want to eat some snacks.Have a glass of water to see if you are thirsty.Sometimes you are just thirsty and you need no food.If you are really hungry，you can have some food.But remember to give up eating before you feel full.
