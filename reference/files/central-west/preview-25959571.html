A
Plants give us food，clothes and oxygen （氧气）.There are over335，000 kinds of plants in the world.Many plants have six parts.During the growth of plants，each part has its own job to do.
A plant's life cycle （周期） begins with seeds.Seeds come in many shapes and sizes.Plant the seeds in the ground and care for them，and then they will grow into plants over time.
The roots of a plant usually grow underground.They take in nutrients （吸收养分） and water for the plant from the earth.
The stem grows up from the roots and helps the plant to stand.It stores nutrients and transports water to other parts of the plant.
Leaves grow out of the stem.The leaves collect sunlight andCO2 during the day and then these things are converted （转化） into sugar and oxygen.
Many flowers are colourful.They help the plant to produce seeds.
Some plants grow fruits which have seeds in them，such as apples and corn.They are all fruits of plants.

Reporter：Did you take design classes after that？
Constance：Yes，I took design classes in college.In my final year，I applied （申请） for graduate school at New York University.There，I studied design.
Reporter：Are costume designers all good at drawing？
Constance：Well，drawing is important，but it's mainly a tool.The key（关键） is to express your ideas clearly.
Reporter：How do you come up with the ideas for costumes？
Constance：I have to do a lot of research about the opera.For example，Who wrote it？Who are the characters？What was going on in the world at that time？By answering these questions，I can better understand the background（背景） of the opera.Then I can work with the director and the rest of the team.
Reporter：What do you love most about your job？
Constance：I always get to meet new people and start new stories.This job keeps me learning！
