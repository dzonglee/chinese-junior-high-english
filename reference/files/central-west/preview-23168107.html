Keep to a set meal routine

Keep to the same pattern (模式) of eating every day
If you are someone who snacks, try to snack at around the same time each day. Try planning when you intend (计划) to eat and check at the end of the day if you have got this.
Focus on your food
Focusing on your food will help you control(控制) the amount(数量) of your food. And if you eat while doing something else, such as walking, it is easy to eat much more than you realize. The following tips may help you focus on your food and help you eat less:
★Eat your meals at the table. It will help you focus on the amount of food you eat.
★Eat slowly. It takes time for your body to realize how much food you’ve eaten and how full you are.
★Don’t eat while walking. Wait until you can sit down and take time to focus on what you are eating.
