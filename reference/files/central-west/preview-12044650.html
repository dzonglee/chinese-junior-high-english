The white stick has been used by many blind people for a long time. However, it has seen no real improvement, until a blind engineer named Kursat decided to make a difference. He hoped his invention would help the blind.

Now, these problems have been solved by WeWalk. “I believe life of the blind will be much easier and safer.” Kursat said.

Hope in my heart
I had trouble learning English before, I got bad grades, so I even lost heart. My teacher had a long talk with me, and I knew I should keep hope in my heart. I found confidence again. So I kept on practicing English every day and working hard on it. Each time I had problems in English, I went to my teacher and asked for the solutions.
About half a year later, I caught up with others, and I got an A in the final exam. I felt so happy. I think if you have hope in your heart, you will be able to face the difficulties bravely and succeed one day.
