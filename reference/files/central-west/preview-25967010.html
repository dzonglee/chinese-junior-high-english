57.【答案】My Healthy Campus Life
As more and more teenagers focus on healthy living，I also have a healthy habit.My healthy habit is doing eye exercises between classes.It only takes a few minutes but helps a lot.Before，my eyes often felt tired from long study.Now，I do the exercises every day.They relax my eyes and make me feel refreshed.I can focus better in class.Also，I feel less stressed because I take short breaks.
I hope all students can try this simple habit.Let's start with small steps，like doing eye exercises or having light meals.A healthy life begins with good habits!
