第三节 书面表达（10分）
My First Try
Last summer, I decided to learn how to ride a bike. At first, I was excited but nervous, holding the handlebars tightly while my dad held the back of the bike. However, as soon as he let go, I lost balance and fell. My knees hurt, but I didn’t give up.
I kept practicing every day, focusing on keeping steady and looking ahead. Gradually, I could ride a short distance without help. One afternoon, I finally rode smoothly around the park, feeling the wind on my face.
This experience taught me that success comes from persistence. Even though the first try was hard, the joy of overcoming fear and achieving my goal was unforgettable. It reminds me that every challenge is a chance to grow.
评分要点：
1. 完整叙述第一次尝试的经历（如学骑自行车），包括过程和困难。
2. 体现坚持后的收获或感受（如成功来自坚持、克服恐惧等）。
3. 语言流畅，语法错误少，词数符合要求。
