Tom is in Grade Seven. Every month, Tom, his mom, and his dad have a special cooking game in the kitchen. Everyone has to make a special food.
Tom’s mom is very good at making salads. She prepares some tomatoes, potatoes, and carrots. She cuts them and puts them together. Then she puts some chicken and sauce (酱汁). The salad looks beautiful and tastes great!
Tom’s dad loves to cook chicken. Because this is Tom’s favourite food. He learns it from Tom’s mom. But Dad has a special sauce to make the chicken more delicious.
Tom can only make a pizza. It is easy. He just needs to put something he likes on the pizza, and bake (烘烤) it. His pizza smells amazing and tastes delicious!
After they all finish cooking, they taste each other’s food and choose the best one. Most of the time, Tom’s mom wins, but everyone has a great time in this game.
Tom loves this cooking game. It’s an interesting way for the family to spend time together and enjoy delicious food.
