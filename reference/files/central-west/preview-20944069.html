A geomagnetic storm comes from a solar storm (太阳风暴) on the sun. Then a lot of materials (物质) from the surface (表面) of the star travel together in space.
When they come to Earth, they start a geomagnetic storm. Their strong magnetic field (磁场) changes Earth’s magnetic environment. It also affects (影响) things in space, such as satellites (卫星) and space stations.
As a result, the storm can bring problems to GPS. So when you check where you are on your phone, it might not be quite right. The storm also affects how well the phones and satellite TVs work. Really strong magnetic storms can cause trouble for power transmission (电力传输), said Professor Cai Zheng from Tsinghua University.
People talked about geomagnetic storms online. Some said they had trouble sleeping, saying it was because of the storm. However, experts later explained that the storm wasn’t the reason. Geomagnetic storms usually don’t affect our sleep and health.

【详解】句意：昨天我们看了一部激动人心的电影。这部电影是关于太空生活的。
考查冠词辨析。第一空需要不定冠词表示泛指，exciting是元音音素开头，an符合句意；第二空再次提及上文的movie，用定冠词the，故选B。
2．D
【详解】句意：——你最喜欢云南的哪个城市 ——事实上，我喜欢云南几乎每一个美丽的城市。
考查副词辨析。Secondly其次；Especially尤其；Heavily沉重地；Actually事实上。根据“I like almost every beautiful city in Yunnan”可知，事实上，我喜欢云南每一个美丽的城市；其他选项均不符合题意，故选D。
