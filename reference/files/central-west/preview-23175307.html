Chinese seals are mainly used to mark important things.Usually, they are carved(雕刻) from stones, but they can also be made of wood, bamboo or bones.They have to be put in red ink(油墨) before using them.
Seals can be carved in one or two ways: the material is carved away from the characters, leaving red ink on paper.Or the characters are carved on the material, leaving the characters white on a red background.
Today, seals are widely used all over China for marking important papers.Seals are often used with names.Chinese paintings are still marked with a personal seal.Most Chinese people have a personal name seal.Artists and learners usually collect full sets of name seals.Seals are very important for businessmen when they deal with their business and show their social positions.They often make a seal to wish they have a smooth business.
In a word, seal carving is an important part of Chinese culture, which is considered as a traditional Chinese art.
