六、One possible version
A Meaningful Vacation
The summer vacation is coming soon.How can we make it meaningful Here are my
plans.
As the saying goes,"Reading makes a full man."So firstly,I plan to do some reading
after I finish my homework.Secondly,I'm going to do more exercise to keep healthy.Also,I
will develop healthy eating habits.Thirdly,I will help my mother with housework,such as
cooking and doing some cleaning.What's more,I will always remember to be safe in daily life
and also try to help others.
In a word,I will make full use of time to spend a meaningful vacation.
