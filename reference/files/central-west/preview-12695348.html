My name’s Li Li, one of the volunteers working in Chengdu Research Base.I am standing here to share something about pandas and my volunteer work.
As everyone knows, pandas are pretty cute and shy. They prefer eating nearly all day long. However, pandas are endangered now. It’s difficult for them to have many babies. What’s more, the environment is not good for them because of human activities.
To change the situations of pandas, I have been a volunteer for years. I sweep the floor and feed the pandas with bamboos. Sometimes I tell children the importance of protecting pandas. Also, I take part in planting trees to make the environment friendlier.
Come to volunteer here just like me！I am waiting for you to join in.
Thanks for listening!
