Last term, I met a big challenge in my physics study. Instead of giving up, I made a detailed study plan and stuck to it every day. I asked my teacher for help when I was confused and reviewed my mistakes carefully. Little by little, my grades improved.
This power teaches me that persistence can help me overcome any difficulty. It makes me feel confident and brave. With the spirit of Qiji, I believe I will keep moving forward unstoppably.
