In our lives, friendship is like a bright star. It lights up our way forward.
There are several useful ways to keep friendship long.
First, good communication matters most. When talking with friends, we should listen to them carefully and express our true thoughts freely. Besides, we must never lie to each other, because honesty is the base of friendship.
Second, we should give a hand to friends whenever they are in trouble. If they fall behind in study or meet difficulties in daily life, we can offer help at once.

If we do these things well, our friendship will last forever.
