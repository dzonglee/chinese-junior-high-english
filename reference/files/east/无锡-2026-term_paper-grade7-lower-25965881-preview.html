Hi，I am Lin Wei.I like cooking Chinese food very much.I started the blog in 2023 to share Chinese food.I'm happy to welcome you here!
The history of Lanzhou beef noodles
Do you like Lanzhou beef noodles？They come from Lanzhou，Gansu.Over 100 years ago，a cook called Ma Baozi made the dish famous on the streets of Lanzhou.And then，Lanzhou noodles became well-known around the world.Now，Lanzhou plans to make a place called the Lanzhou Beef Noodles Industrial Park.This place will bring many businesses together about making or selling beef noodles.
The way to make Lanzhou beef noodles
Prepare for the soup
★Prepare some ingredients（原料），like bones and meat.
★Boil（煮沸） to clean them.
★Cook them slowly for 2-3 hours.Prepare for the noodles
★Make the dough（面团）
★Knead the dough
★Roll into strips
★Stretch the noodles
The unusual things about the noodles
Lanzhou beef noodles are famous for their"five colours"：one clear —the fresh beef soup，two white —soft radish pieces，three red —spicy oil，four green —fresh green onions，and five yellow—hand-made noodles.All these make the noodles look nice and taste great.That's why people all over the world lovethem.

Far away in the mountains，there was a small village.A girl named Annie lived there with her parents.The village was close to a river，the villagers' favourite place—children played in its cool water in summer，and every family got their drinking water from it.However，things suddenly changed one hot summer.Months passed with almost no rain at all.And the water level in the river fell lower and lower every day.Annie got very worried：would the river dry up？
One night，she couldn't sleep well and kept thinking about the river."▲，"she said to herself.The next morning，she shared her ideas with her parents.They began collecting rainwater，and they used it to water their garden.They also started reusing water.For example，after they finished washing the dishes，they put the water in a bucket（桶） and used it to flush the toilet（冲厕所）.By the end of the month，Annie and her family managed to save quite a lot of water.
Annie knew that if the whole village worked together，they could save much more water.She was a little worried if the villagers would listen to her，but she hoped to have a try.
At a village meeting，Annie shared her ideas.There were many leaking（漏的） taps（水龙头）and water pipes（水管） in the village.Annie told the other villagers，"If we find all the leaks and fix them，we can save hundreds of buckets of water.And every drop we save can help keep our river alive."The villagers smiled and agreed.They checked all the taps and pipes together and helped each other fix them.
That summer，everyone in the village worked together as a big family to save water.
When the first autumn rain finally came，the villagers had a big party，and they thanked Annie with a bunch of beautiful flowers，"What a resourceful girl!"

26. A long time ago，Chinese children had no mobile phones，iPads or computers to play with.But they still had great fun playing many interesting games in their daily life.Different games were popular in different places，and here are some classic ones for you to know.
During the Qing Dynasty，boys in northern China loved playing stone balls with their feet.They cut hard stones into small balls，about the same size as a chicken egg.They usually played the game in cold winter，because running and kicking could help them keep warm easily.When the game started，they tried their best to keep the ball around themselves，and not to lose it to other kids.
Hide-and-seek（捉迷藏） was popular across the whole country.Most of the time，some children found secret places to hide，and one child tried his best to find all of them.It was so fun that almost every child loved it.
Watching shadow plays was also a favourite activity.It started over 2，000 years ago，and became very popular in the Tang and Song Dynasties.Like today's films，it told wonderful stories with puppets（木偶） behind a cloth，with light and music.
Playing diabolo（空竹） was popular in North China.It was made of wood or bamboo.Kids played it with two sticks and a string（线），and it made a loud sound when rotating（旋转） fast.It was also a good way to exercise.
