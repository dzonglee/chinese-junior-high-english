16、Every day after school，Ethan would rush home，get his guitar and lose himself in the music.Playing the guitar was his greatest joy.
One day，a local talent show was going to start.Ethan decided to join，but soon he started to worry.He compared himself with other musicians and began to question his skills.The fear of failure began to weigh on him.
Ethan's grandfather noticed Ethan was troubled.He then shared a story from when he was young."I once dreamed of being a painter，but self-doubt held me back，"he said."I went to see an old artist who told me to paint a picture in front of a crowd.I was scared and didn't know what to do."
The artist smiled and said，"Don't worry about what others think.Paint from your heart.The real beauty comes from being true，not being perfect."With this advice，Ethan's grandfather let his feelings guide him.The crowd watched in amazement as a beautiful picture took shape.
Inspired（启发）by his grandfather's story，Ethan practiced tirelessly.On the day of the talent show，he walked onto the stage（舞台） and played like never before.His fingers danced over the strings（弦），creating music that touched listeners' hearts.
Ethan didn't win the talent show，but he felt like a winner.He learned that success isn't just about winning a prize—it's about enjoying what you love to do.The true reward（奖励） is the journey itself.

Located in the heart of New Zealand's North Island，the Tongariro Alpine Crossing is seen as the most iconic day hike for people who love adventure and nature.
When you start this amazing journey，you'll see that it's more than just a hike.In the Tongariro National Park，a UNESCO World Heritage site，the path offers a special chance to explore the natural beauty of New Zealand's environment.
What makes this hike special is its different geological features，including the active volcanoes（火山） of Tongariro，Ngauruhoe and Ruapehu.You will see bright blue lakes，empty rocky fields and green plants all in one single day.
The path has clear signs and is well kept.People of different fitness abilities can enjoy it，but they still need to be strong enough for a long walk.Most hikers finish the hike in 6 to 8 hours，depending on how fit they are and how many breaks they take.
It's also important to choose the right time for your hike.The best months for hiking are during the summer season，which runs from December to February in New Zealand.During this period，the weather is not changing much and the days are longer，giving you enough time to complete the hike in daylight.
Spring and autumn，known as the shoulder seasons，can be wonderful times for hiking.They often have fewer crowds.You can enjoy fantastic views，especially in autumn when the leaves change color.
Before you go，remember to check the weather because it can change quickly in the mountains.
Whether you are an experienced hiker or a new walker，you'll find each step of the journey meaningful.

As the Palace Museum celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2025，it is still a symbol of Chinese culture that connects the past and the present.Its collection covers thousands of years and many art forms，including paintings，Chinese writing art as well as daily objects once used by emperors.These treasures are now a common cultural resource（资源），open to the public.
However，protecting these artworks took years of effort.When the Japanese army got close to the Great Wall in 1933，the museum organized efforts to move nearly 20，000 boxes of artworks south，and then west to Sichuan for safety.They finally returned to Beijing after October 1949，showing the nation's dedication（投入） to cultural protection.
While based on tradition，the Palace Museum continues to involve（创新）.Technology now helps protect special artworks and improve the tourists' experience.Digital records protect art for the future，while online and VR tours let people around the world explore the museum easily.
Today，the Palace Museum is "growing over younger"，according to China Daily.Cultural products designed by the museum have become popular among young travelers."As most visitors to these ancient palaces are young people，it is living proof that a hundred years is not a process of aging，but a process of renewal，"noted China Daily.
