A healthy coral reef is one of the noisiest places in the ocean.Scientists can tell if it's healthy by listening to it.A healthy coral reef should be full of sounds made by fish.When some coral reefs died in 2015 and 2016，they became quiet.After people set up the protected areas，scientists heard the animals come back.
