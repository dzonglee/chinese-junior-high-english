Do you know what people usually eat on the winter solstice (冬至) and why
Once upon a time, there was a doctor named Zhang Zhongjing. Dr Zhang saw lots of poor people in his city. It was very cold and people there had no hats to keep their ears warm, so they had red and sore (痛的) ears. The winter solstice came. It is the shortest day of the year. It has the longest and coldest night. Dr Zhang was kind and warm-hearted. He wanted to help poor people feel better and decided to make something warm for them to eat. He took a pot of hot water outside. First, he put mutton and vegetables in the pot. Then he began to make dough (面团). After that, he made the dough into small circles. He put the mutton and vegetables inside. He folded them and made them into an ear shape! At last, he put the “dough ears” in the soup. He gave them to the people in the city. After eating, they felt warm. Even their ears were warm!
All of the people thanked Dr Zhang. Then people learned to make “dough ears” on that day. Later, its name was changed into jiaozi or dumplings. It has a great wish for warmness and health.

Special Food Full of Memories
There is a special dish in my heart, and it is filled with unforgettable memories. It’s my grandma’s handmade dumplings.
Every time I visit my grandma during the winter holiday, she makes dumplings for me. The smell of the freshly cooked dumplings fills the whole kitchen. I still remember how she taught me to wrap them, though my dumplings always looked funny. Sitting around the table, we talked and laughed. That warm feeling is something I will never forget.
To me, these dumplings are not just delicious food—they are love, family, and home.
