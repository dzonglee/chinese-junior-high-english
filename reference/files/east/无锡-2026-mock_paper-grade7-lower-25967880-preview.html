2.请认真核对监考教师在答题卡上所粘贴条形码的姓名、考试证号是否与本人相符合,再将自己的姓名、考试证号用0. 5毫米黑色墨水签字笔填写在答题卡及本试卷上。
3.答选择题必须用2B铅笔将答题卡上对应的答案标号涂黑。如需改动,请用橡皮擦干净后,再选涂其他答案。答非选择题必须用0. 5毫米黑色墨水签字笔写在答题卡的指定位置,在其他位置答题一律无效。
第I卷（客观题 共50分）
一、单项选择 在A、B、C、D四个选项中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项，并在答题卡上将该项涂黑。（本大题共14小题，每小题1分，共14分）
1．Which of the following underlined parts is different in pronunciation from the others
A．Sharks. B．Lions. C．Monkeys. D．Toads.
【答案】A
【详解】句意：下列划线部分中，哪一个的发音与其他选项不同？
Sharks/ ɑ ks/；Lions/ la nz/；Monkeys/ m kiz/；Toads/t dz/。Sharks中-s发/s/，其他选项中-s一般发/z/，发音不同的是Sharks。
2．He likes playing ________ basketball, but he can also play ________ erhu.
A．an; / B．an; a C．a; / D．/ ; the
【答案】D
【详解】句意：他喜欢打篮球，但他也会拉二胡。
play后接球类运动名词时不加冠词，后接乐器名词时需加定冠词the。basketball是球类运动，前面不加冠词；erhu是乐器，前面加the。应填/ ; the。
3．It’s too late. I ________ leave (离开) now.
A．may B．can C．have to D．will
【答案】C
【详解】句意：太晚了。我现在不得不离开了。
may可能；can能；have to不得不；will将要。根据“It’s too late.”可知时间太晚，表示客观情况迫使必须离开。故选C。
4．Look! The sky is dark. It ________ soon.
A．rains B．is going to rain C．rained D．was raining
【答案】B
【详解】句意：看！天空变暗了。很快就要下雨了。
rains下雨（一般现在时）；is going to rain将要下雨（一般将来时）；rained下雨（一般过去时）；was raining正在下雨（过去进行时）。根据“The sky is dark”及“soon”可知，此处表示根据现有迹象推测即将发生的事，用be going to符合语境。
5．It’s snowing outside. Please put on your ________ to keep warm.
A．T-shirt B．coat C．skirt D．sunglasses
【答案】B
【详解】句意：外面正在下雪。请穿上你的外套来保暖。
T-shirtT恤衫；coat外套；skirt裙子；sunglasses太阳镜。根据“It’s snowing outside”及“keep warm”可知，下雪天为了保暖应该穿外套。应填coat。
6．—How’s your weekend going
—_______. I have a cold and feel bad.
A．Great B．Terrible C．OK D．Sure
【答案】B
【详解】句意：——你周末过得怎么样？——糟透了。我感冒了，感觉很不舒服。
Great很棒；Terrible糟糕的；OK还不错；Sure当然。根据“I have a cold and feel bad.”可知，回答者感冒了，感觉不舒服，说明周末过得很糟糕，应填Terrible。
7．Koalas are very lazy, ________ they sleep all day.
A．so B．but C．or D．because
【答案】A
【详解】句意：考拉非常懒，所以它们整天睡觉。
so所以；but但是；or或者；because因为。根据“Koalas are very lazy”可知考拉懒是原因，“they sleep all day”是结果，前后构成因果关系，且前因后果，应用so连接。所以选A。
8．Could you please help me ________ the pictures on the wall, Mike
A．put up B．put on C．put away D．put off
【答案】A
【详解】句意：迈克，你能帮我把画张贴在墙上吗？
put up张贴、悬挂；put on穿上；put away收起来；put off推迟。根据“Could you please help me…the pictures on the wall”可知，此处表示把画挂在墙上，应用put up。应填put up。
9．We ________ our team because we won the football match yesterday.
A．were proud of B．were afraid of C．were tired of D．were full of
【答案】A
【详解】句意：我们为我们的队伍感到骄傲，因为我们昨天赢了足球比赛。
were proud of为……感到自豪；were afraid of害怕；were tired of厌倦；were full of充满。根据“because we won the football match yesterday”可知，赢了比赛应该感到自豪。
10．—What do I need to do if I want to make egg fried rice
—________ you need to cook the eggs. Then you can pour the rice into it.
A．In fact B．In the end C．First D．At times
【答案】C
【详解】句意：——如果我想做蛋炒饭需要做什么？——首先你需要炒鸡蛋。然后你可以把米饭倒进去。
In fact事实上；In the end最后；First首先；At times有时。根据后句“Then you can pour the rice into it.”可知，此处描述做饭的步骤，强调第一步操作，应填First。
11．My grandpa goes for a walk in the park ________ and this is his ________ exercise to keep healthy.
A．everyday; every day B．every day; every day
C．every day; everyday D．everyday; everyday
【答案】C
【详解】句意：我爷爷每天在公园散步，这是他保持健康的每日锻炼。
every day每天，是副词短语，表示频率；everyday日常的，是形容词，修饰名词。第一个空表示散步的频率，用every day；第二个空修饰名词exercise，用everyday。故选C。
12．—_________ does the book cost
—$50. A little bit expensive.
A．How many B．How soon C．How much D．How far
【答案】C
【详解】句意：——这本书多少钱？——50美元。有点贵。
How many多少（可数名词）；How soon多久，多快；How much多少（不可数名词），多少钱；How far多远。根据答语“$50. A little bit expensive.”可知，回答的是价格，问句应询问价格，应用How much。
13．Don’t worry. We can take care of ________ at the school camp.
A．ourselves B．themselves C．yourself D．itself
【答案】A
【详解】句意：别担心。在学校露营期间我们能照顾好我们自己。
ourselves我们自己；themselves他们自己；yourself你自己；itself它自己。根据主语“We”可知是能照顾好“我们自己”，用ourselves。
14．—Is the movie ________
—Yes, I’m really ________ in it.
A．interesting; interested B．interesting; interesting
C．interested; interested D．interested; interesting
【答案】A
【详解】句意：——这部电影有趣吗？——是的，我真的对它很感兴趣。
interesting有趣的，通常修饰事物；interested感兴趣的，通常修饰人。第一空主语是“the movie”，指物，用interesting；第二空主语是“I”，指人，且be interested in为固定短语，用interested。

阅读下面的语篇，从各小题所给的A、B、C三个选项中，选出最佳选项。

16．A．seriously B．brightly C．carefully
17．A．description B．pollution C．attention
18．A．sea B．lake C．river
19．A．rubbish B．fruit C．fish
20．A．nervous B．bored C．happy
21．A．studying B．saving C．creating
22．A．pick up B．look after C．throw away

24．A．find B．set C．hit
【答案】
15．C 16．A 17．B 18．C 19．A 20．C 21．B 22．A 23．B 24．C
【导语】本文主要讲述了Daniel从7岁起就关注河边塑料垃圾问题，13岁时联合朋友成立“Cleanup Kids”组织，号召全球孩子参与清理河流垃圾、保护环境的故事。
【详解】15．句意：我知道塑料瓶和其他垃圾可能对河里的动物有害。
上文“I felt worried”表明，Daniel对垃圾持担忧态度，说明垃圾对动物是有害的，harmful“有害的”符合语境，good“好的”、helpful“有帮助的”与担忧语义矛盾。
16．句意：塑料和其他垃圾严重污染了田纳西河。
修饰polluted应选表示程度的副词，seriously“严重地”符合语境，brightly“明亮地”、carefully“仔细地”与污染搭配不通。
17．句意：我想做些事来帮助阻止更多的污染。
上文提到河水已被污染，此处应是阻止更多“污染”，pollution“污染”符合语境，description“描述”、attention“注意”不符。
18．句意：我和其他人一起做了一些河流清理活动。
全文围绕“riverbank”“rivers”“Tennessee River”展开，应是河流清理，river“河流”符合语境，sea“海”、lake“湖”不符。
19．句意：我们经常发现大量垃圾，比如坏掉的玩具和塑料袋。
下文“such as broken toys and plastic bags”是垃圾的举例，rubbish“垃圾”符合语境，fruit“水果”、fish“鱼”不符。
20．句意：做清理让我真的很开心。
下文“I know I’m making a difference”表明Daniel感到有成就感、开心，happy“开心的”符合语境，nervous“紧张的”、bored“无聊的”不符。
21．句意：我知道我正在改变并拯救生命——不只是动物的生命，也包括人类的生命。
上文“making a difference”及下文“not just animal lives, but human lives as well”表明是在拯救生命，saving“拯救”符合语境，studying“研究”、creating“创造”不符。
22．句意：号召全世界的孩子捡起垃圾。
全文主题是清理垃圾，pick up“捡起”符合语境，look after“照顾”、throw away“扔掉”与清理目标相反。
23．句意：人们拍下垃圾的照片并发送给我们。
“take pictures”和“send them”是先后顺承动作，应用and连接，and“和、并且”符合语境，or“或者”表选择，but“但是”表转折，均不符。
24．句意：我们将在本月底达到100万件的目标。
hit the goal“达成目标”，固定搭配，hit“达到”符合语境，find“找到”、set“设定”不符。

Butterflies are among the most beautiful insects on the earth. Their colorful wings, flying from one flower to another, bring fun to people of all ages. However, they are in big trouble now. Since 2000 the number of butterflies goes down all the time.
Collin Edwards, who works at the Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife, does a study on this worrying situation. Volunteers play an important role in understanding the situation. They love butterflies and help monitor them. Monitoring here means carefully watching and taking notes of their numbers, living places, and behaviors. This helps scientists like Edwards find out what’s going on.
Three main things let the number of butterflies go down. First, because cities grow and people cut down forests, butterflies’ homes are destroyed. Second, pesticides are a big problem. People use pesticides to kill bad insects, but they are also bad for butterflies and other helpful insects. Third, weather change is making things worse. The Earth is getting warmer. This changes the places where butterflies can live. Some of the plants might die because of the warmer weather.
There is something we can do to protect the butterflies. A simple way is to grow flowers. They are good for butterflies in our gardens. These flowers not only give butterflies food but also give them a safe place. We can also tell people to use fewer pesticides and protect butterflies’ living places. By doing these things, we can help save the butterflies.

Butterflies are among the most beautiful insects on the earth. Their colorful wings, flying from one flower to another, bring fun to people of all ages. However, they are in big trouble now. Since 2000 the number of butterflies goes down all the time.
Collin Edwards, who works at the Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife, does a study on this worrying situation. Volunteers play an important role in understanding the situation. They love butterflies and help monitor them. Monitoring here means carefully watching and taking notes of their numbers, living places, and behaviors. This helps scientists like Edwards find out what’s going on.
Three main things let the number of butterflies go down. First, because cities grow and people cut down forests, butterflies’ homes are destroyed. Second, pesticides are a big problem. People use pesticides to kill bad insects, but they are also bad for butterflies and other helpful insects. Third, weather change is making things worse. The Earth is getting warmer. This changes the places where butterflies can live. Some of the plants might die because of the warmer weather.
There is something we can do to protect the butterflies. A simple way is to grow flowers. They are good for butterflies in our gardens. These flowers not only give butterflies food but also give them a safe place. We can also tell people to use fewer pesticides and protect butterflies’ living places. By doing these things, we can help save the butterflies.

Every day, many people go to work or school by bike or on foot. Traffic rules are very important for everyone’s safety.
