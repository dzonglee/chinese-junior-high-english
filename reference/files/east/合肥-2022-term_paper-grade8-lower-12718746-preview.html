【解答】A依赖，依靠；B寻找；C化妆，组成；D考虑；结合句意可知，不要过多依赖字典，自己猜测生词意思。

3．（1分）﹣ Pass me my glasses，Danny.I can__________see the words without them.
﹣ OK.Grandpa.（　　）

﹣现在就开始用中文交谈，熟能生巧。
【解答】A．Why为什么；B．when什么时间；C．whether是否；D．how如何。根据Start talking with Chinese now and practice makes perfect.现在就开始用中文交谈，熟能生巧。可知问的是如何讲好普通话。用how提问。
故选：D。
5．（1分）Being an excellent magician is not an easy job even when you__________over and over again.（　　）
A．will practice B．have practiced
C．are practicing D．were practicing.
【分析】成为一名优秀的魔术师并非一件容易的事，甚至当你一遍又一遍地练习时。
【解答】A一般将来时；B现在完成时；C现在进行时；D过去进行时；结合语境可知，句子在陈述一种事实，应该用现在的时态，所以先排除选项AD；反复练习后带来的结果是并非就可以成为一个优秀的魔术师。只有完成时才强调动作产生的影响和结果。
故选：B。

8．（1分）﹣Which one would you like to buy？Bing Dwen Dwen or Shuey Rhon Rhon？
﹣I'd like__________.I have two children.（　　）
A．none B．either C．both D．neither
【分析】—你想买哪一个，冰墩墩还是雪融融？
—我两者都想买。我有两个孩子。
【解答】none没有一个，都不，用于三者或三者以上；either每一个，任何一个，用于两者；both都，用于两者；neither都不，没有一个，用于两者。根据后文"I have two children."（我有两个孩子）可知，一个孩子一个，应该买两个，因此是both。

For the most part，your body will tell you when you need to drink water because you've got these wonderful and surprising things﹣﹣kidneys（ 肾脏）.Your body is an environment where everything needs to be in a good balance（平衡）.If levels of anything get too high or too low，then it is the job of the kidneys to keep them just right.
Imagine you are running for a long time and you're losing water，then it may lead to dehydration.And then you'll do whatever you need to get some water.So thanks to your kidneys，your body is really good at keeping water.
But if you stop counting eight glasses of water a day，how much should you drink？The answer is：there is no should.In fact，everything you have has water﹣﹣an apple has water，so does your breakfast.So as long as you're thirsty，trust your body.
（1）Why does the writer call kidneys wonderful and surprising things in paragraph 3？ 　B　
A.Because they are able to make better decisions.
B.Because they keep everything in our body in a good balance.
C.Because they can make us brighter and clearer.
D.Because they can count how many glasses of water we drink.
（2）What does the underlined word "dehydration" in paragraph 4 mean？ 　A　
A.脱水
B.瞌睡
C.劳损
D.多汗
（3）When should we drink water according to the text？ 　C　
A.After we have breakfast.
B.Before we go for a run.
C.When we feel thirsty.
D.As soon as we get up.
【分析】文章讲了什么时候喝水的问题。
【解答】（1）推理判断题。根据For the most part，your body will tell you when you need to drink water because you've got these wonderful and surprising things﹣﹣kidneys（ 肾脏）.Your body is an environment where everything needs to be in a good balance（平衡）.If levels of anything get too high or too low，then it is the job of the kidneys to keep them just right.（在很大程度上，你的身体会告诉你什么时候需要喝水，因为你有这些奇妙而令人惊讶的东西——肾脏。你的身体是一个环境，一切都需要处于一个良好的平衡。如果任何东西的水平太高或太低，那么肾脏的工作就是保持它们恰到好处。）可知，为什么作者在第3段中称肾脏为奇妙和令人惊讶的东西因为它们使我们体内的一切保持良好的平衡。故选B。
（2）词义猜测题。根据上下文Imagine you are running for a long time and you're losing water，then it may lead to dehydration.And then you'll do whatever you need to get some water.So thanks to your kidneys，your body is really good at keeping water.（想象一下，你跑了很长时间，却失去了水分，那么这可能会导致脱水。然后你会做任何你需要的事情去弄点水。多亏了你的肾脏，你的身体真的很善于保持水分。）可知，第4段中带下划线的单词 "dehydration" "是脱水"的意思。故选A。
（3）细节理解题。根据So as long as you're thirsty，trust your body.（所以只要你口渴，相信你的身体。）可知，根据文章，当我们感到口渴的时候喝水。故选C。
19．（6分）阅读下面短文，并用英语回答问题（请注意每小题后面的词数要求）。
Jenny used to get angry at everything.Once she found if she tried to work out one problem another one followed at once.So she asked her father what to do.
Her father asked Jenny to take a potato，an egg and some tea leaves.He then took three bowls of water and placed them on the cooker.Then he dropped the potato in a bowl，an egg in the second one and the leaves in the third one.
"Why are you doing these？" Jenny asked，really angry with her father.
"Just wait for few minutes，" said her father.The daughter looked even angrier.
Then her father took out of the potato and the egg，and then he put the tea in a cup and left the leaves.
"What do you see？"
"A potato，an egg and a cup of tea，" said Jenny.
"Touch it."
"I still don't understand."
Her father said，"We put them in the same water，but they changed differently.The potato was so hard but now it has turned soft.Also，the egg would break easily but now it is hard and the tea leaves changed the water themselves，giving it a special smell and taste!It gave something Jenny then understood that she can make something new and special even when she experiences difficulties.
（1）Where did her father drop the tea leaves？ （不超过10个词） 　He dropped the tea leaves in the third bowl.　
（2）How did Jenny feel when she was waiting？ （不超过5个词） 　She felt even angrier.　
（3）What can we learn from the story？ （不超过15个词） 　When encountering difficulties，we should learn to blaze new trails.　
【分析】本文主要讲述了Jenny过去对一切都很生气。一旦她发现，如果她试图解决一个问题，另一个问题马上就会出现。于是她父亲
通过生活事例让她明白，即使在她遇到困难的时候，她也可以做出新的和特别的东西。
【解答】（1）细节理解题。根据Then he dropped the potato in a bowl，an egg in the second one and the leaves in the third one.（然后他把土豆丢在一个碗里，第二个碗里有一个鸡蛋，第三个碗里有叶子。）and then he put the tea in a cup and left the leaves.（然后他把茶放在杯子里，留下了叶子。）可知，故填：她父亲把茶叶丢在第三个碗里，故填：He dropped the tea leaves in the third bowl.
（2）细节理解题。根据 "Just wait for few minutes，" said her father.The daughter looked even angrier.（"等几分钟，"她父亲说。女儿看起来更生气了。）可知，Jenny在等的时候感觉更生气，故填：She felt even angrier.
（3）细节推理题。根据Also，the egg would break easily but now it is hard and the tea leaves changed the water themselves，giving it a special smell and taste!It gave something Jenny then understood that she can make something new and special even when she experiences difficulties.（此外，鸡蛋很容易破碎，但现在它变硬了，茶叶自己改变了水，给它一种特殊的气味和味道！它给了珍妮一些东西，然后她明白了，即使在她遇到困难的时候，她也可以做一些新的和特别的东西。）以及通读全文可推理出，我们能从这个故事中学到遇到困难时要学会另辟新径。，故填：When encountering difficulties，we should learn to blaze new trails.

20．（1分）You can't e 　xpect　（期盼） to learn a foreign language in a few days.
【分析】你不能指望在几天内学会一门外语。
【解答】期盼expect，是动词，在情态动词can后面用动词原形。
故答案为：expect。
21．（1分）Playing too many computer games is a w 　aste　（浪费） of time.
【分析】玩太多电脑游戏是浪费时间。
【解答】根据提示"浪费"可知，a waste of..."浪费......"，填单数名词。
故答案为：waste。
22．（1分）The Chinese government has c 　reated　 （创造） more jobs for young people.
【分析】中国政府为年轻人创造了更多的就业机会。
【解答】根据提示"创造"可知，是动词create。由助动词has可知，现在完成时态，结构是have/has+动词的过去分词。create的过去分词是created。
故答案为：created。
23．（1分）It's c 　ertain　（确定的） that he can get good grades with hard work.
【分析】毫无疑问，只要努力学习，他就能取得好成绩。
【解答】根据提示"确定的"可知，这里is系动词，系表结构，填形容词certain作表语。
故答案为：certain。
24．（1分）A h 　ero　（英雄） is never put off by difficulties.
【分析】英雄从不因困难而退缩。
【解答】根据提示"英雄"可知，这里不定冠词A修饰单数名词hero。
