第四部分书面表达（共1小题，满分20分）
76.Possible version(一：
To keep fit,I prefer to exercise with partners.It's a great idea because exercising with friends
can bring me more fun and make me feel less bored.Also,we can encourage each other when we
feel tired or lazy.This way,we not only improve our health but also make our friendship stronger.
Starting from next month,I will exercise with my best friend,Li Hua,three times a week
after school.We will choose different activities,like play ing badminton on Monday,running in the
park on Wednesday,and swimming on weekends.We'll also take photos to see our changes and
cheer for each other every time.I believe if we keeping moving together,we will stay healthy and
happy！
Possible version(二：
To keep fit,I prefer to choose Exercise with partners.The reasons are as follows.
Firstly,having someone to exercise with can help improve our friendship.We chat and laugh
during the exercise,which will make us grow closer to each other.What's more,When I feel tired
and want to stop,my partner will give me a cheer-up.And we can encourage each other all the
way.
As for my future plan,I've made up my mind to keep really fit.I will eat more vegetables
and fruit.Of course,exercise is also very necessary.In a word,no matter which way we choose,
the key is to keep moving and stick to it.Only by making efforts can we achieve our fitness goals
and enjoy a healthy life.
