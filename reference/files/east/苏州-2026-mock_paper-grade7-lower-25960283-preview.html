Forests are made up of trees. Do you know the following types of forests on Earth
Temperate forest (温带森林)
Temperate forests are found all over the world. These forests have four seasons. They change at different times of the year. Many kinds of trees grow in a temperate forest. Their leaves change colours with the seasons. Leaves fall off the trees when the weather gets cool. As the weather warms up, the trees sprout.
Boreal forest (北方针叶林)
Boreal forests are found in northern parts of the world, like North America, Asia, and Europe. Of the three types of forests, boreal forests have the shortest growing season, about 130 days. Winters are extremely cold, making it difficult for life. The largest boreal forest is in Russia (mainly in its Asian part). Animals there get used to the cold weather by growing thick fur (厚厚的皮毛).
Tropical rainforest (热带雨林)
You may guess that it rains a lot in a tropical rainforest. You are right! Tropical rainforests are hot and wet. These forests cover only a tenth of the Earth, yet they are home to more kinds of plants and animals than any other places. The biggest tropical rainforest is the Amazon. It is in South America and it’s home to 10% of the world’s plants and animals.
Trees give us oxygen. They help clean the air. Cutting down too many trees will hurt the environment (环境).

【导语】本文是一篇寓言故事，讲述了一个贫穷的旅行者通过制作“石头汤”，用智慧巧妙引导不愿分享的村民拿出食材，揭示了“分享能带来快乐，团结与分享让生活更美好”的道理。
【详解】1．句意：一天，一个贫穷的旅行者到了。
根据“because he didn't have any money for meals”（因为他没钱吃饭），可知旅行者没钱，是贫穷的（poor）。rich富有的；lazy懒惰的；shy害羞的，均不符合没钱这一语境，故选B。
2．句意：他在一所房子前停下，敲了敲门。
根据“the door”及想要进屋求助的语境，固定搭配“knock at the door”表示敲门。picked up捡起；pointed to指向；waited for等待，均不符合求助场景，故选C。
3．句意：但是老人对他说“不”。
根据“But”表示转折，且前文提到村民不喜欢分享，及老人拒绝给食物，say no to sb.表示拒绝。good好；OK好的，表示同意，hello你好，表示问候，均不符语义，故选C。
4．句意：然后旅行者有了一个主意。
根据下文他捡起石头并提议做汤的行为，可知他想出了一个办法。have an idea是固定搭配。answer答案；example例子；interest兴趣均不符合语境，故选A。
5．句意：你想尝尝石头汤吗？
根据“stone soup”及“It’s very delicious”，可知是邀请老人品尝，have soup表示喝汤。blow吹；pour倒；buy买均不符合品尝食物的语境，故选B。
6．句意：老人很惊讶，问道：“石头汤？怎么做？”

7．句意：只要给我一些热水和一个锅就行。
根据下文“The old man gave him water and a pot”，可知旅行者请求老人给予（give）物品。sell卖；teach教；show展示均不符合索取物品的语境，故选A。
8．句意：但是如果有一些盐会更美味。
根据“if there is some salt”，加盐是为了改善食物味道。important重要的；famous著名的；strange奇怪的均不符合烹饪加调料的目的，故选C。
9．句意：如果我们有一些蔬菜，比如胡萝卜和西红柿，那就完美了。

第六部分 阅读表达（共3小题；54题2分，55题2分，56题3分，满分7分）
阅读短文回答问题，每题答案不超过4个单词
Ink brushes (毛笔) are important in Chinese culture. They are used in paintings and writings.
Xu Wenyi comes from Wenzhou. He runs an ink brush shop with his two brothers. His grandfather starts the shop in 1900. His father learns the skill at 13 and later runs the shop. Xu also begins learning at the same age. He and his brothers are the third generation (代).
There are about 40 steps to make an ink brush. The first step is to choose the hair. Every winter, Xu goes to other cities to collect the best animal hair. Next, he and his workers make the hair tidy. This takes days or even a week for one small strand (缕). Then they cut the hair into different sizes. These days, the brothers try new things. They make lanugo (胎毛) brushes with babies’ hair, and many new parents enjoy them. They also show brush-making in their shop and at cultural festivals. Many culture lovers show great interest in the skill.
“We are happy to make good brushes,” Xu says. “We want to pass on the skills. We believe we can make better brushes by spreading the skill.”
54．When does Xu Wenyi begin to learn to make ink brushes
55．What is the first step to make an ink brush
56．How can Xu make better brushes
【答案】54．At 13. 55．Choose the hair. 56．By spreading the skill.
【导语】本文介绍了温州许文义一家三代传承毛笔制作技艺的故事，包括毛笔的制作步骤和他们的创新尝试。
54．第二段提到许文义和父亲一样，也是在13岁开始学习毛笔制作“Xu also begins learning at the same age”，结合前文父亲13岁学艺，可知答案是At 13。
55．第三段提到制作毛笔的第一步是挑选毛发“The first step is to choose the hair”，答案是原文直接信息。
56．最后一段提到许文义认为通过传播技艺可以做出更好的毛笔“by spreading the skill”，答案是原文直接信息。

57．假设你是Tommy，上周末你们班组织了一次户外活动。请根据下表提供的信息写一篇80词左右的短文介绍这次户外活动。

（2） 注意人称和时态。（文章开头已给出，不计入总词数）
Hello, I’m Tommy. Everybody knows outdoor activities help us get close to nature and relax.
_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
【答案】例文
Hello, I’m Tommy. Everybody knows outdoor activities help us get close to nature and relax. Last weekend, our class organized a cycling activity.
We set off at 8 a.m. last Sunday. During the ride, we ran into a problem: one of my classmates’ bikes had a flat tire. We stopped to help him repair it. Although we were a bit tired, we felt excited and proud. From this activity, I learned that teamwork is very important.
It was a meaningful experience, and I’m looking forward to the next outdoor activity.

②时态：时态以“一般过去时”为主；
