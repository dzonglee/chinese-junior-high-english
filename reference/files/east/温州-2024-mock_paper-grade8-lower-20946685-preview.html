My friend
Hello! My name is Tony. I have a good friend. His name is Simon. He is 14 years old. He is a Chinese boy. He studies in No.3 Middle School. He is in Class 2 Grade 7. He likes playing all kinds of sports, because he thinks doing sports is good for his health. He often has vegetables, fish, beef and orange juice for lunch. He likes geography, but he doesn't like math, because he thinks it's difficult.
Best wishes!
