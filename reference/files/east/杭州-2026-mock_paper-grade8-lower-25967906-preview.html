Recently, the world has been excited about a new AI model, DeepSeek-R1. It was developed by DeepSeek, a Chinese business in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province. The new AI came out on Jan 20. In just one week, it beat OpenAI’s ChatGPT and became the top free app in Apple’s App Store.
The developers said that DeepSeek-R1 is as good at difficult tasks as other big models but needs less computing power (算力). It is also the cheapest of its kind and took only about 5.57 million US dollars (40.58 million yuan) to develop, while US businesses have had to spend much more money building similar AI models.
DeepSeek-R1’s success largely came from its special training method. Traditionally, AI is trained in two ways. One is Chain-of-Thought (CoT). In this way, AI learns to break problems into smaller and simpler steps. The other is Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT). It works by showing AI lots of problems together with the right answers for it to find a pattern (模式). DeepSeek-R1, however, used Reinforcement Learning (RL) as the key method. In RL, AI learns by trying different things. It gets rewards for correct actions and gets punished for mistakes. For example, in a game, it gets points for picking up rubbish and loses points for breaking a glass. Over time, it works out the best solution (解决方法) all by itself.
In short, DeepSeek-R1 is great. This model shows that China is getting better at AI. DeepSeek-R1 tells us that new ideas can make AI grow, and it will help many parts of our life.

45．句意：他邀请了Wei Ning、付星、高老师和其他很多同学。
举办派对需要邀请宾客，应用invited“邀请”，符合。harmed“伤害”，accepted“接受”，taught“教”，均不符合派对场景。
46．句意：他们聊天、欢笑，享用美味的食物，同时了解彼此的风俗习惯。
派对上享用美食，应用enjoyed“享受、享用”。sold“售卖”，grew“种植”，picked“采摘”，均不符合派对场景。
47．句意：很多人都喜欢Eric友善的举动，和他成为了好朋友，包括Wei Ning和高老师。
Eric主动办派对弥补过失、促进交流，是友善的行为，应用kind“友善的”。interesting“有趣的”，private“私人的”，wrong“错误的”，均不符合对该行为的评价。
48．句意：通过这次经历，Eric学到了中式肢体语言的用法，也学会了如何做到礼貌得体。
全文是一次跨文化交流的经历，应用experience“经历”。result“结果”，decision“决定”，report“报告”，均不符合语境。
49．句意：他的室友和同学们都明白，当人们有着不同的习俗时，保持耐心和开放的心态非常重要。
面对文化差异，需要包容开放的态度，应用open-minded“思想开放的”。hard-working“勤奋的”，warm-hearted“热心的”，good-looking“好看的”，均不能对应文化差异的相处原则。
50．句意：他们都变得更加亲近，证明了友谊与理解能将人们联结在一起。
经过交流与理解，大家的关系更近了，应用closer“更亲近的”。smarter“更聪明的”，quieter“更安静的”，luckier“更幸运的”，均不符合人际关系的变化。

【详解】51．句意：我们都想要彻底揭开它的奥秘，可是该从何入手呢？此处修饰动词“unlock”，需要用副词形式。方框中的“full”是形容词，变为副词“fully”，表示“完全地”，符合语境。

53．句意：什么生物生活在幽深未知的深海之中？探索通常是针对未知的事物。方框中的“known”表示“已知的”，加否定前缀un-变为unknown，符合探索未知的语境。
54．句意：即便付出艰辛努力，自然界仍有许多奥秘未能破解。此处位于句首，后接名词短语“hard work”，需要填入介词，此处表示“即便”，方框中without的反义词with符合语境。
55．句意：每一小步都帮助我们离大自然的重大奥秘更近一步。此处指“自然界的”秘密。方框中的“nature”是名词，变为所有格形式nature’s。

56．roles 57．pilots 58．challenging 59．remind 60．effort 61．disappointed 62．choice 63．sweat 64．completely 65．career

第三节 语法填空（共10小题，每小题1分，满分10分）
阅读下面短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。
As we all know, health is important to everyone. 66 (improve) children’s health, primary and middle schools in Shenzhen offer one PE class every day. So far this action 67 (make) a difference. A mother says, “My fourth-grade daughter used to dislike exercising. Although she hated sports at first, since the introduction of daily PE classes, she has shown 68 interest in running. At first, I encouraged her 69 (keep) on running every day. But after running for a couple of 70 (month), she has fallen in love with running. And her health has improved a lot.”
Some parents worry that children may give up running 71 (easy). However, experts suggest that students won’t develop a lasting habit 72 schools create regular activities like daily PE classes and running clubs.
A physical training coach believes that running is a good form of physical training available to young children. “With comfortable sports shoes, students can run anytime and anywhere.”
Third-grader Yu Rongtao does physical training outdoors every week. His mother says, “Last month, he 73 (join) a school running club. Now he often leads his friends in the morning runs, and it has become one of the 74 (happy) parts of their daily life.”
According to physical training coaches, whatever sport children choose, it is of great 75 (important) for them to experience the benefits of exercise and develop a habit.
【答案】
66．To improve 67．has made 68．an 69．to keep 70．months 71．easily 72．unless 73．joined 74．happiest 75．importance
【导语】本文讲述了深圳中小学通过每日体育课提升学生健康水平的举措，以学生和家长的真实经历为例，说明了坚持跑步对孩子健康的积极影响，并强调了学校活动对培养运动习惯的重要性。
【详解】66．句意：为了改善孩子们的健康，深圳的中小学每天开设一节体育课。此处用不定式表目的，improve的不定式形式是To improve，句首首字母大写
67．句意：到目前为止，这项行动已经产生了影响。so far是现在完成时的标志，主语this action为单数，make的现在完成时形式是has made。
68．句意：自从开设每日体育课以来，她对跑步产生了兴趣。固定搭配show an interest in，表示“对……表现出兴趣”，填不定冠词an。
69．句意：一开始，我鼓励她每天坚持跑步。固定搭配encourage sb. to do sth.，表示“鼓励某人做某事”，用不定式to keep。
70．句意：但是跑了几个月后，她爱上了跑步。a couple of后接可数名词复数，month的复数形式是months。
71．句意：一些家长担心孩子们可能会轻易放弃跑步。此处用副词修饰动词短语give up，easy的副词形式是easily。
72．句意：然而，专家表示，除非学校开展日常体育课和跑步俱乐部等定期活动，否则学生不会养成持久的习惯。此处表示“除非”，用连词unless。
73．句意：上个月，他加入了学校的跑步俱乐部。last month是一般过去时的标志，join的过去式是joined 。
74．句意：现在他经常在晨跑中带领他的朋友，这已经成为他们日常生活中最快乐的部分之一。one of后接形容词最高级，happy的最高级是happiest。
75．句意：对于孩子们来说，体验锻炼的好处并养成习惯是非常重要的。固定搭配be of great importance，表示“非常重要”，important的名词形式是importance。
