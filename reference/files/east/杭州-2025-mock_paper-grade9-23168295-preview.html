I'm going to finish my junior high school this summer. In my class, some students plan to travel in the summer vacation, others prefer to have part-time jobs.
As for me, I prefer to travel with my classmates. I plan to go to Beijing and visit some places of interest. First, we can relax ourselves completely. Last but not least, traveling is a good way to make the friendship strong. I will have more sweet memories with my friends.
