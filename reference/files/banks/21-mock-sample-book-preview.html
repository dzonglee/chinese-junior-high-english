【导语】本文讲述了美国纽约克拉伦斯中学的学生参加2024年“为水而走”活动，通过模拟乌干达儿童取水的艰难过程来筹集资金，帮助乌干达民众获取清洁水源。过去十年该校已筹集大量资金，用于建设雨水收集池等，改善了乌干达学校的用水状况。

Know myself, Become a better myself
Self-management is important to us. It means managing ourselves, such as our time, our feelings and so on. In the future, we will soon graduate and become a college student. So we should first learn to manage our time in order to make full use of it. For example, I will try my best to listen to the teacher carefully. What’s more, I will also learn to manage my money. I should wisely spend the pocket money given by my parents.
As for the plan, I will learn how to communicate with my parents. When I disagree with them, I’ll try my best to understand them. Besides, I’ll take part in some after-school activities and work as a volunteer to gain more social experience.
In a word, I believe I will be a better me in the future.
