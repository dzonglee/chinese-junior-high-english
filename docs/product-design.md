# Product behavior and current scope

The app helps mainland junior-high learners decide what to practise, try it independently,
understand the reason for an answer, and return after a delay. It is a native WeChat app with a
browser verification harness. It does not present completion or self-marking as mastery.

## First use and routing

The learner confirms city, six-three/five-four structure, numeric grade, term, school-year start
and exact textbook/version. No city adoption is guessed. Switching structure clears incompatible
choices rather than silently choosing a textbook. Shanghai's preparatory label and five-four
local labels retain the same underlying numeric grades. A backup can be imported before filling
out a fresh profile.

Only a confirmed examination date creates a countdown. Cohort matching uses the selected school
year and grade, not a guessed June date or the device timezone. Current unit mappings and local
blueprints are unverified in the runtime catalog, so the interface visibly offers generic
capability practice. Research collection and commercial permissions are separate from this app.

## Daily rhythm and courses

Today's plan prioritizes due vocabulary, mistakes and delayed lessons. New-word warmup follows
the next interactive course. The chosen daily time is a planning target: tasks beyond it are
labelled as additional practice rather than silently increasing the target. The hero and course
list always let learners continue a course. Interrupted and completed sessions remain accessible
in practice history (the latest 40 detailed sessions).

A lesson begins with an action: choose, type, reorder, correct or express an idea. The learner
checks an answer before advancing. Feedback contains the answer, a strategy, reasoned steps,
individual distractor explanations, a concrete pitfall and a meaningful sentence/evidence diagram.
Retry and hint use are preserved. Delayed review uses distinct items/passages and is spaced after
recent exposure rather than allowing overdue stages back to back.

## Practice

The builder filters the exact recorded unit, knowledge point, skill, response kind and difficulty.
It shows the usable pool count, respects grade, excludes unplayable listening and refuses to
repeat items to meet an impossible count. An unverified unit is not invented to populate a picker.

Supported original practice includes vocabulary recall, contextual grammar, sentence ordering and
correction, cloze, reading evidence/inference/main idea, sentence restoration, short answers,
listening, free speaking/read-aloud and writing. Listening answers and transcript evidence appear
after checking; audio must be played before ordinary listening feedback. Full timed practice
hides solutions and hints until submission, supports question navigation and submits at deadline.
Writing/speaking marks require explicit self-review and remain separate from objective scores.
A verified local mock also requires an official exact-city/structure/cohort blueprint.

The word review asks for recall before reveal. Pronunciation/example controls appear after the
attempt when a matching approved recording exists. Speaking uses explicit microphone permission,
record/save/playback/delete controls and a self-check rubric. There is no automatic speech score.
Writing drafts save on input and survive navigation/restart. Private questions require the same
explanation structure and remain separate from the public authored bank.

## Progress and data

Independent attempts, assisted practice and self-review have separate evidence types. An old
solution peek or a hint followed by a new session cannot become independent credit. Spelling
practice, wrong-answer retries and delayed review follow the same principle. Retention needs a
successful delayed response, not just a lesson checkmark.

Mistakes have an error cause, due date and scheduled retry streak. Early successful practice does
not advance the delayed streak. Learners can remove an item manually without gaining mastery.
History, favourites, progress, editable settings, privacy, export/import and deletion are reachable
from the profile. Portable backups omit audio files and preserve existing device recordings on
import. Complete deletion also clears orphaned saved audio and temporary app-owned backups.

## Launch scope still needing an owner decision

The current original collection has 34 lessons, 368 questions and 96 vocabulary entries across
all six skills. Twelve listening scripts still need approved audio. This is a foundation collection,
not complete 1,600-word coverage or textbook-by-unit coverage. The owner must decide whether this
scope is the initial capability release or whether more original content and verified mappings
are required before launch. No researcher claim or release gate is marked complete by these counts.

Colors, cards and touch targets follow the sister maths design direction. Decorative art comes
only from the reproducible Blender pipeline. Browser screenshots are labelled browser evidence;
native DevTools, device, privacy configuration and editorial release remain explicit prerequisites.
