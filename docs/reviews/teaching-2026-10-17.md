The owner's requirement is that students learn by doing, with short prompts and clear feedback.

not editorial release approval or evidence of measured learning effectiveness.

attempt or lesson pass. Distinct delayed tests remain unexposed. Speaking/writing use explicit
self-review; text length and checked criteria do not establish semantic or pronunciation accuracy.
