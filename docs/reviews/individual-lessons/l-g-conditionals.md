# l-g-conditionals · switch the hypothesis and follow the plan

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

An original diptych shows indoor reading in rain and garden observation in dry weather. They
are alternative hypothetical plans, never a forecast or chronological pair. Tapping a weather
condition changes the actual displayed outcome. A wrong requested hypothesis turns red while a
correct tense and the consistent alternative outcome remain blue.

Eight tasks separate conditional present tense, the future main-clause verb, reversed clause
position, unless, future when, a modal result and if meaning whether. Four require typed forms.
The negative condition accepts both doesn't rain and does not rain, including normal case and
curly apostrophe variants. Unsupported tense/agreement forms remain incorrect. The compiler
checks accepted alternatives against exact reserved sentences too.
