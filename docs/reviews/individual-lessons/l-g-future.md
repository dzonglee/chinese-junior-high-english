# l-g-future · plan, promise, question and predict

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

The cleanup picture is deliberately reused as a completed prior event. A new coded record supplies
the separate future poster plan: a finished activity in the picture does not make every connected
sentence past tense. Selecting a record moves its time marker; unsupported chronology cannot pass.

Seven tasks cover will make, will send with a singular subject, will be ready, won't leave,
Will ... bring, I am going to make and a prediction using current dark-cloud evidence. The last
task requires the present observation, not a future umbrella plan or last week's rain. Evidence
time and predicted event time are explicitly distinct. Going-to tasks have a specified structure;
the lesson does not falsely label an otherwise valid will alternative wrong.

## Whole lesson

Prediction now concerns tomorrow's poster. Distinct application tasks require will visit and
will make. A fresh reading-event transfer checks Will ... be ready / She will send, including
be in questions and the absence of third-person s after will. Each wrong option has its own
reason. Delayed items remain unexposed and assisted work grants no assessment records.

## Inspected evidence

All seven models, wrong-record recovery, application and transfer were inspected. Review clarified
that Amy's future camera arrangement is still unconfirmed, shortened the going-to explanation,
and made the temporal-record prompt precise. The complete browser path and 21 task × width checks
