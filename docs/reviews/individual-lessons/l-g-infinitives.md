# l-g-infinitives · connect the form to what happens

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

An original two-panel picture contrasts drinking with a closed book and reading with a capped
bottle. Learners tap the later action that fits the supplied earlier action, then choose stop
to drink / stop drinking. The pictures alone do not establish time or purpose; explicit records
do. Selecting the wrong picture leaves an otherwise correct expression blue and blocks completion.

Nine authored tasks cover this meaning contrast, want/decide, the performer after ask, a negative
tell request, purpose, too…to and remembering a future action. Three expressions are typed. The
science-room art locates a stated wish to make another windmill; an existing finished model does
not prove a future wish. Teacher and performer remain separate in the request diagram.
