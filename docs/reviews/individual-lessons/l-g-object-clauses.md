# l-g-object-clauses · edit the clause inside the sentence

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

Eight bespoke tasks turn direct questions into editable embedded clauses. Students add individual
word tiles to the actual sentence, remove misplaced words, discard unneeded auxiliaries and
restore agreement. The engine derives the expression from selected tile IDs; whole-answer choose
or input actions cannot bypass assembly. Duplicate words retain distinct identities. Edits clear
the saved completion, and incomplete repairs survive reload.

The sequence covers where/is order, do-support and starts/costs, if/whether, subject who, a stated
past viewpoint, whether or not, and optional that. Valid variants are explicitly accepted and
built through the same controls. A compact original book/umbrella picture grounds the first
location question; it does not prove ownership. Required premise selection stays separate from
word order. Expandable diagrams compare the original with the learner's actual expression.
