# l-g-continuous · identify an actor and complete the ongoing action

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

The original generated display scene supports holding a book, holding a paper plane and watering
a plant. It does not establish reading or folding; those claims were deliberately rejected after
inspecting the image. Code supplies the current time, names and speaker. Students must locate the
actor and choose the complete predicate, rather than click an isolated -ing form.

Six scene tasks contrast singular names, speaker I, an and-group and a subject identified by
with a watering can. Wrong people are marked separately from wrong predicates. The final two
written records use live word-building diagrams for make → making and run → running. Actual
attempts such as makeing and runing stay visible. These written records describe another group;
the illustration is not used to assert unsupported actions.

## Whole lesson

Prediction uses the book-holding scene. Distinct application tasks correct someone knocking and
report children cleaning. A fresh two-clause transfer requires is running / are making, checking
subject agreement, the complete be + ing predicate and both spelling changes. Every distractor
has its own explanation. Original delayed items remain unexposed; no guided action grants marks,
mastery or a lesson pass.

## Inspected evidence

All eight correct models, wrong actor/predicate, both wrong word forms, application, clinic and
fresh transfer were inspected. Review removed repeated goals and redundant time-record text.
The generated water stream supports watering; the plane is already formed, supporting holding.
The complete real-control path and 24 task × width checks pass, including reload restoration and
zero independent assessment records. This is a local browser review, not native GUI approval.
