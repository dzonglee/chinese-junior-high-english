# l-g-conjunctions · preserve the relationship when the sentence changes

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

Eight tasks link reasons, results, concession, contrast, otherwise and conditions. The inspected
original weather picture supports two scenarios; the requested rainy one explains indoor reading.
The same causal diagram stays in the same direction when English changes from result/because/cause
to cause/so/result. Explicit 所以 and 却 labels distinguish consequence from contrast rather than
making every diagram look causal.

Two tasks use editable words to remove duplicated because/so and although/but in the requested
formal clause structures. Other tasks require the correct scenario or relation before accepting
the expression. Although keeps a result contrary to the original concern; otherwise presents a
possible consequence, and the gloves example states a sufficient participation condition. No
fictional activity rule is presented as a general school policy.
