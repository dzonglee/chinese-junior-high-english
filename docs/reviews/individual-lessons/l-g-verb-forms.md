Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

a past passive, took/taken, e removal, final-consonant doubling and unchanged put. Students type
forms and repair actual makeing/stoped drafts. Cue-to-form diagrams show the learner's attempt;
they never add the answer to itself. Written records distinguish completed past events from now.
The past passive postcard is explicitly from yesterday, avoiding a contradiction with current writing.

then choose writing. A correct form cannot pass with the wrong picture. The same fictional cast
connects the new activity with the travel-diary lesson. No generated text supplies an answer.

to identify the form after has. Explanations remain available after a genuine attempt.
