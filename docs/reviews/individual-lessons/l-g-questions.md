## Teaching decision

auxiliary and reference. The five-day calendar requires all actual walking days, not any three
choices. Minutes and kilometres are different quantities. A silent original class photo requires
selecting both girls before using they. The tag examples cover lexical present/past verbs, be,
present perfect, negative never, Let's and the common contracted aren't I form.

The final task supplies Mia's actual positive preference and asks her to answer a negative tag.
A grammatical No answer is still wrong if it contradicts the given fact. Wrong evidence stays
separate from an otherwise correctly built English response. Guided completion grants no
independent attempt, mastery or lesson pass.
