# l-g-past · reconstruct a completed event from its record

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

An inspected original diptych contrasts classmates cleaning with the completed result. Code adds
panel labels and fictional dated records; an image alone cannot establish yesterday. Students
select the record that supports the sentence. Their choice moves the event marker, and a correct
verb cannot bypass wrong chronology. Correct verb forms stay blue when only the record is wrong.

Seven tasks cover a completed cleanup, go → went, a cancelled previous activity with didn't clean,
a Did ... go question, past singular/plural states was/were, and stop → stopped. The two Sundays
have distinct outcomes; cancelled and completed activities are not conflated. Negative/questions
leave the lexical verb in its base form. The word-building diagram retains stoped until repaired.

## Whole lesson

Prediction uses the cleanup. Distinct applications require bought and answered; a fresh library
transfer requires went / stay after didn't. The explanation checks irregular forms separately
from the auxiliary rule. Original delayed items remain untouched. Guided completion creates no
independent attempts, mastery or lesson pass.
