# l-g-possessives · see who owns what, then place the apostrophe

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

Nine tasks distinguish one owner, regular and irregular plural owners, shared and separate
belongings, time expressions and the possessive pronoun its. Students edit real wrong forms,
including student's/students', childrens', the two album arrangements and hour's/hours'.

The album diagram is bespoke: Mia and Leo join to one shared book, or each connects to their
own book. The learner's selected relationship determines the actual allocation. Wrong selections
remain visible in red even when the typed possessive is correct. No choice means no invented
allocation. Correct letters alone cannot pass with the wrong relationship. Book centres are
checked against their owners at320/390/768 pixels. Names are checked with exact case.

Owner counters also change with the selected singular/plural referent. All quantities are explicit:
one or two students, two children sharing one art corner, one child, one shared album versus two
separate albums, and one versus two hours. A visual review removed misleading default book icons
beside grammar words; only actual albums receive book symbols. its is labelled as a possessive
pronoun, not a noun's possessive form. Immediate question feedback uses Chinese 撇号 and ’s.
