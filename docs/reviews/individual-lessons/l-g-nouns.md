# l-g-nouns · count objects and units, then type the noun

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

Every task requires actual typing. Deterministic pictures guarantee exact counts: three then one
sandwich, two milk bottles, three children, two leaves and three bread slices. The counter does
not silently correct the learner's word. Quantity changes produce a singular/plural contrast while
the object stays the same.

Ten tasks cover sandwiches/sandwich, uncountable advice, bottles of milk, children, leaves,
photos versus tomatoes, uncountable bread despite three visible slices, and pieces of advice.
Classification is required where counting objects/units could be confused with the content noun.
The model retains errors such as sandwichs, advices, photoes, tomatos and breads. Hints name the
base word; the optional reference is learner-controlled. The leaf explanation does not falsely
generalize its f/ves change to every f-ending noun.

## Whole lesson

The original picnic prediction is retained. Distinct applications practise advice and bottles
in different tasks. Fresh camp advice requires pieces / advice, checking the plural unit separately
from its uncountable content. Each distractor has a reason. Original delayed items remain untouched;
guided typing is never recorded as independent assessment, mastery or a lesson pass.
