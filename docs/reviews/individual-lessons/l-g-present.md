# l-g-present · report a habit, then build the verb

Authoring date: 2026-10-18 (Sydney). Content remains draft. Individual visual/control review passed.

## Teaching decision

A walking snapshot cannot establish a daily habit. The original walking scene is paired with
an explicit fictional four-week weekday record. The student identifies the grammatical subject,
then reports the habit using the present simple. The complete subject matters more than the
number of people visible or the noun nearest the gap.

Four scene tasks contrast `Mia walks`, `I walk`, `Mia and Leo walk`, and
`Mia, with her friends, walks`. The last two deliberately use the same group but different
subject structure. Selecting everyone who is walking does not answer which people the subject
refers to. Feedback distinguishes a wrong referent from a wrong verb.

Four spelling tasks then address separate mistakes: `finish → finishes`, `study → studies`,
`play → plays`, and `go → goes`. The result node shows the student's actual attempted form;
`studys`, `plaies` or `gos` is not silently replaced by the answer. The study model uses a rewrite
arrow because removing y and adding ies is different from simply appending s. Captions no
longer print the complete answer before the student tries. Only the current verb's choices show.

## Whole lesson

Prediction now uses Mia's weekday walk. Application practises a distinct `finish` correction and
plural `teachers ... ask`. The separate transfer contrasts `The boy with two bags ...` with
`His friends ...`, addressing misleading nearby plural nouns. Each distractor has its own reason.
Original delayed questions remain unexposed and no guided work grants assessment records.

## Review evidence

The eight tasks and their incorrect/correct model states were rendered and inspected. Review
removed answer-revealing captions, removed duplicated plus signs, and changed the study editing
step to rewrite arrows. Role labels moved off faces; weekday text is larger and shorter. Delivery
images use 768-pixel derivatives; original generated files and their provenance are retained.

The actual browser path passed all eight tasks, 24 condition × width checks, distinct application,
clinic, fresh transfer and summary, with no assessment records. Typecheck, all 1,450 core tests,
826 spelling models and package checks pass. These are bounded local checks, not editorial or
native approval. [Control results](l-g-present/controls.json).
