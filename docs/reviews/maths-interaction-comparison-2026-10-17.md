older Blender-only instruction in AGENTS.md. The single generated asset is an empty blue/white
desk, inspected before use. Book geometry, position and English labels are rendered by code, so

in `content/visual-assets.json`. Original Blender assets remain in use elsewhere.

No public release, audio model, video production, exact textbook map or editorial approval is
inferred. Audio remains pending and videos remain deferred. An interactive foundation has been
added; 13 workshops do not establish the maths app's required interaction coverage in all 413
lessons. Remaining teaching work is to author and review a domain-specific exploration for every
lesson sequence, especially reading inference, listening and multi-stage writing revision.
