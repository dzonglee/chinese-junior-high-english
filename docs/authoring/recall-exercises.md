# Guided cover–recall–compare exercises

A proof/card item may opt into `sourceRecall` with a title, an honest note and two to four
original cue/answer pairs. Stable word IDs follow `[a-z][a-z0-9-]*` and must be unique.
This mechanism is separate from source gaps, order, calculations and source illustrations.

The study phase shows the reference. Hide removes reference answers from the rendered recall
view. Each cue takes an actual saved attempt; students may write that they do not remember.
Reveal requires a nonempty attempt in every field and places the references beside unchanged
attempts. Case-insensitive word-form comparison is a prompt for self-review, not a semantic or
retention score. Wrong recall can be recorded as a learning gap. No independent attempts,
mastery, listening credit or lesson passes are created.

Editing any attempt hides all references again and invalidates the checked source conclusion
and completion. Restudying or rehiding also invalidates completion without discarding drafts.
Completion requires the comparison phase, actual source evidence and saved reasoning. Existing
source guards remain in force. Restore applies actual storage validation; stale completion flags
cannot bypass the required phase or empty attempts.

New exercises must be checked through web input, native component input updates, both native
adapter modes, actual storage, offline navigation, and phone/tablet geometry. Inspect study,
empty recall, incorrect comparison, edited recall, matched forms and restudy states. Reference
words may be present in content data; this is guided practice, not a secure independent test.

In l-ls-course-t04, map/bridge/corner are an explicitly original demonstration list, not words
reported in the dialogue. Exact code visibility is the teaching visual. Do not add decorative
art or suggest immediate success establishes later retention. Approved audio remains separate.
