A proof card task may use `sourceArt` to make a quoted choice visible. Keep exact labels and answer states in code; generated pixels cannot establish identities, permission, measurements or completed actions. Record provenance in `content/visual-assets.json`, inspect the original and delivery image, and inspect correct/wrong/repaired phone states.

`sourceArt.assetRoot` may be `assets` or `illustrations`; omitting it preserves the existing `illustrations` path. The compiler requires the asset ID to match its registered native file exactly. Arbitrary paths are rejected. Both roots are named subpackages loaded before application readiness, with independent failure/retry checks and unchanged size budgets. Browser and standalone images use the registered asset lookup. Do not duplicate an image to satisfy a hard-coded path.
